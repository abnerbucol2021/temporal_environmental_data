ISIMIP3a historical population extraction
============================================================
Data class: historical_population_reconstruction
Resolution: 5 arcmin
Period: 1900-2018
Buffers: 25, 50, 70 km
Buffer method: grid-cell-center haversine inclusion
Population measure: sum of gridded population values
Time handling: raw numeric annual offsets decoded manually from 'years since YYYY...' because xarray does not support years as a CF datetime unit.
